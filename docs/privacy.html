PDF Tools Offline is designed to perform PDF compression, merging, page editing, batch processing, and image conversion entirely on your device. This policy explains what the app does—and, importantly, does not do—with your information.

Information we collect

PDF Tools Offline does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not operate a backend service.

The app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or behavioral tracking technology, and we operate no separate analytics service for it. The app does not send us device identifiers, document names, document content, or usage events.

If you install the app through Apple, Apple may make privacy-protected, aggregate App Store analytics and crash or diagnostic reports available to the developer when users have opted to share that information in their device settings. Apple collects and controls that information under its own terms; it is not collected by an analytics or tracking SDK in PDF Tools Offline.

How your documents are handled

When you select a PDF or images through an Apple system picker, the app creates working copies inside its private local app container. Compression, merging, thumbnails, page editing, numbering, image conversion, and output validation happen on the device.

The app does not upload your source or output files. Finished PDFs remain local until you choose where to save or share them.

Your choice matters after processing.

If you share a PDF with another app or cloud provider, that third party’s privacy terms apply to the copy you send.

Apple system services

PDF Tools Offline uses standard Apple system interfaces, including file and photo pickers, save panels, and sharing controls. These interfaces are provided by iOS and macOS. The app requests access only to files and images you explicitly select.

Your use of iCloud Drive, third-party file providers, or sharing destinations is governed by Apple and the provider you choose. PDF Tools Offline does not receive information about your accounts with those services.

Local storage and retention

Working copies and generated files are stored in the app’s local sandbox so the requested task can be completed and the result can be saved or shared. Temporary working files are cleared automatically the next time the app launches. iOS may also remove cached files when storage is needed.

Deleting the app removes its local app container. A file you separately saved or shared outside the app is controlled by the destination you selected.

Children’s privacy

Because the app does not collect personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app’s features or data practices change. The updated policy will show a new “Last updated” date. If a future version introduces any collection or network service, this policy and the app’s disclosures will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be submitted through the project’s GitHub support channel. Please do not attach confidential PDFs or personal documents to a public issue.
