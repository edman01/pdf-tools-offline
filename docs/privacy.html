PDF Tools Offline is designed to perform PDF compression, merging, page editing, batch processing, and image conversion entirely on your device. This policy explains what the app does—and, importantly, does not do—with your information.

Information we collect

The app has no developer-operated document server or account system. Your PDFs, images, filenames and document content are not sent to the developer or the advertising service.

The iPhone/iPad version includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform for consent. When permitted, ads may involve IP addresses (including approximate location), device or app identifiers, diagnostics, performance information, ad impressions and interactions. We request non-personalized ads and disable publisher first-party ID; this does not mean the SDK collects no data. We do not request Apple's tracking permission or use document content for targeting.

Consent messages are shown where required. You can revisit Ad Privacy Options in Settings when available. All PDF tools work even when ads cannot load or you do not grant advertising consent. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's SDK data disclosures for its practices and retention.

You can remove ads with a non-consumable, one-time App Store purchase. StoreKit verifies purchase status with Apple; the app does not receive your payment-card details. A verified ad-removal purchase prevents further banner requests and hides existing banners. It can be restored using the same Apple Account. The native Mac version currently contains no advertising SDK.

Apple may provide opt-in App Store analytics and diagnostics under your device settings. Apple handles this information under its own terms.

How your documents are handled

When you select a PDF or images through an Apple system picker, the app creates working copies inside its private local app container. Compression, merging, thumbnails, page editing, numbering, image conversion, and output validation happen on the device.

The app does not upload your source or output files. Finished PDFs remain local until you choose where to save or share them.

Your choice matters after processing.

If you share a PDF with another app or cloud provider, that third party’s privacy terms apply to the copy you send.

Apple system services

PDF Tools Offline uses standard Apple system interfaces, including file and photo pickers, save panels, and sharing controls. These interfaces are provided by iOS, iPadOS, and macOS. The app requests access only to files and images you explicitly select.

Your use of iCloud Drive, third-party file providers, or sharing destinations is governed by Apple and the provider you choose. PDF Tools Offline does not receive information about your accounts with those services.

Local storage and retention

Working copies and generated files are stored in the app’s local sandbox so the requested task can be completed and the result can be saved or shared. Temporary working files are cleared automatically the next time the app launches. The operating system may also remove cached files when storage is needed.

Deleting the app removes its local app container. A file you separately saved or shared outside the app is controlled by the destination you selected.

Children’s privacy

This is a general-purpose document utility, not a service directed at children. Advertising and consent settings must match the app's intended audience and applicable age requirements. Please contact us if you have a question about a child's information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app’s features or data practices change. The updated policy will show a new “Last updated” date. Changes to advertising, purchases or data practices are reflected here and in the app's store disclosures before release.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be submitted through the project’s GitHub support channel. Please do not attach confidential PDFs or personal documents to a public issue.
